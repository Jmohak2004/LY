SuryaRakshak: India Heatwave Surveillance

Live Monitored Heatwave Locations and Risk Management

SuryaRakshak is India's leading smart heatwave surveillance and risk intelligence platform. We provide live heatwave tracking, thermal stress monitoring, and district-level risk intelligence for Indian regions to help manage extreme heat crises. Our mission is to empower municipal bodies, health responders, and citizens with actionable climate intelligence. We offer 7-day heatwave forecasts, hourly thermal trends, and a comprehensive district vulnerability index based on population density, socio-economic factors, and thermal exposure. With our real-time monitoring dashboard, you can stay ahead of severe thermal stress conditions across multiple districts and states. We provide actionable health and safety advisories specifically designed for citizens, health responders, and municipal workers to mitigate the effects of extreme temperatures. Additionally, our platform connects you to a real-time directory of cooling centers, relief camps, and emergency hydration shelters with active occupancy tracking.

Through advanced multi-year satellite thermal telemetry provided by Open-Meteo and NASA POWER RE Community datasets, our climate intelligence dashboard allows for 30-day heat anomaly comparisons to better understand regional risk escalation. Extreme heat is a silent killer, and proactive surveillance is key to mitigating casualties. SuryaRakshak's escalation matrix tracks high-risk zones, giving state authorities the critical data they need to deploy emergency resources efficiently. Monitor temperature, humidity, computed heat index, and the vulnerable population count per district in one unified, easy-to-use interface. Explore our resources to ensure heat safety for your community today.

Heatwaves are becoming more frequent and intense across South Asia. Our intelligent monitoring system aggregates meteorological data to issue early warnings, preventing heatstroke and other heat-related illnesses. We believe that access to accurate, timely thermal data is a fundamental requirement for public safety. Join us in building a more resilient, climate-ready future for all.
